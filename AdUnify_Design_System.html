Design System · v1.0

AdUnify — a quiet, premium canvas for reading ad performance.

Ivory and card white as the base, aged gold as the single accent that carries meaning, and a small jewel-tone set — burgundy and emerald — reserved for telling platforms apart in charts. No shadows, no gradients, no rounded-card kit: structure comes from hairline rule and a thin colored edge, the way a private ledger separates its sections.
